Community Relations & Operations

Building Relationships. Coordinating Programs. Supporting the Mission.

Community relations and operational coordination were central components of my work with U.S. Army Public Affairs at Fort Leonard Wood, Missouri.

My responsibilities involved connecting military organizations with civilian communities, coordinating public engagements, supporting installation activities, and working with leadership and stakeholders to ensure programs were effectively planned and executed.

My work required balancing community needs, organizational priorities, military operational requirements, and communication objectives.

Featured Case Study 1

Student Leadership Award Program

Program Development | Community Partnerships | Recognition Framework

Research & Planning

In the post-COVID period, Fort Leonard Wood needed a consistent way to rebuild relationships with local schools. I developed a monthly recognition program centered on Army Values and positive student impact.

Implementation

Created the nomination and recognition framework; coordinated school administrators, materials, scheduling, and senior-leader participation; and prepared clear, community-focused messaging for presentations.

Evaluation & Impact

The program recognized 200+ students across 15+ school districts, restored recurring engagement with regional schools, and created a sustainable platform for positive community storytelling.

Featured Case Study 2

Read Across America Campaign

Community Outreach | Program Scaling | Volunteer Mobilization

360+ Soldier Volunteers
940 Classrooms Reached
18,000+ Children Engaged
15+ School Districts
Research & Planning

When assuming management in 2020, participation was limited to one district. Developed a scalable model that expanded school participation while preserving consistent messaging and manageable coordination.

Implementation

Recruited and scheduled 360+ Soldier volunteers across program years; coordinated schools, classrooms, units, and senior leaders; and adapted the 2021 program to virtual delivery through produced reading videos.

Evaluation

The campaign grew to reach 18,000+ students in 940 classrooms across 15+ schools and districts, becoming one of the installation's largest annual outreach initiatives.

Verified Published Coverage: Fort Leonard Wood celebrates Read Across America Day (Army.mil)
