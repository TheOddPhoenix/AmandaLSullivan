Building Relationships. Coordinating Programs. Supporting the Mission.

Community Relations & Operations

Community relations and operational coordination were central components of my work with the U.S. Army Public Affairs Office at Fort Leonard Wood, Missouri.

My responsibilities involved connecting military organizations with civilian communities, coordinating public engagements, supporting installation activities, and working with leadership and stakeholders to ensure initiatives were effectively planned and executed.

Throughout my career, I coordinated more than 200 community engagements and supported programs involving schools, civic organizations, military units, government agencies, and members of the public.

This work required balancing community needs, organizational priorities, military operational requirements, and public affairs objectives.

Community Relations & Public Engagement

I served as a point of coordination between Fort Leonard Wood and surrounding communities, supporting requests for military participation in civic, educational, and public events. My work included coordinating military participation in parades, ceremonies, school visits, recognition programs, and other community activities.

Responsibilities included evaluating requests, coordinating participation with military units, communicating with event organizers, preparing participants, managing logistics, and ensuring engagements aligned with organizational priorities and applicable requirements.

Public Affairs Operations

In addition to community outreach, I supported the operational functions of a government public affairs office serving a large military installation.

  • Coordinating public affairs support for official events and ceremonies.
  • Communicating with military units and external organizations.
  • Preparing informational materials and pre-engagement briefings.
  • Supporting media engagements and public information activities.
  • Managing public inquiries and coordinating responses.
  • Maintaining records, digital assets, and communication materials.
  • Supporting event scheduling and logistical coordination.
  • Preparing after-action reports documenting outcomes and lessons learned.
