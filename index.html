Qualitative Research • Organizational Communication • Analysis

AI in the Workplace Focus-Group Project

Challenge: Explore how professionals working in Department of Defense STEM environments understand the opportunities, concerns, and organizational implications of artificial intelligence in the workplace.

Audience: Academic research advisors, organizational leaders, and DoD STEM professionals.

Amanda’s Role: Recruited participants with comparable professional backgrounds, developed and used a moderator guide, moderated the focus group, reviewed and coded the transcript, grouped findings into themes, analyzed patterns across participant responses, prepared a formal report, and presented findings to the client.

Strategy & Deliverables: Constructed qualitative inquiry protocols, executed structured group moderation, performed thematic coding analysis, and synthesized findings into an actionable report and executive slide deck.

Verified Outcome

  • Academic client research project delivered with actionable empirical findings on organizational AI perception and communication gaps

What it demonstrates: Qualitative research rigor, thematic analysis, objective reporting, and strategic communication insights.

About Amanda

Amanda Sullivan

[ Headshot Image Placeholder ]

Amanda Sullivan is an award-winning communications professional with experience spanning public affairs, journalism, photography, multimedia production, organizational communication, digital content, stakeholder engagement, and program operations.

During her Department of the Army career, she translated complex missions and organizational priorities into communication that connected leaders, service members, families, media, and surrounding communities. Her work included strategic campaigns, crisis communication, feature writing, official photography, multimedia production, executive messaging, editorial planning, community outreach, digital publishing, and high-visibility events.

Amanda is completing a Bachelor of Arts in English with a concentration in Professional Communication and Journalism through the University of Maine at Presque Isle. Her work combines strategic thinking, operational experience, visual judgment, and a belief that effective communication should be clear, accurate, useful, and human.

“I have never been interested in communication that merely fills space. I want to understand what needs to be said, who needs to hear it, and what form will make it matter.”

How I Work
