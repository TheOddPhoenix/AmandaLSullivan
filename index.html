About Amanda Sullivan

Professional Biography & Competencies

I'm Amanda Sullivan, a public affairs and communications professional with nearly eight years of experience supporting the U.S. Army at Fort Leonard Wood, Missouri.

My professional background encompasses public relations, strategic communications, community relations, event planning and operations, journalism, photography, multimedia production, and program coordination.

Throughout my career, I've worked with military leadership, civilian organizations, government personnel, educational institutions, and community stakeholders to support public information initiatives, coordinate community engagement programs, and develop communication materials for diverse audiences.

My work has included coordinating more than 200 community engagements, developing regional outreach initiatives, producing award-winning photography, writing published news and feature articles, and supporting the operational responsibilities of a large government public affairs office.

Beyond my government communications career, I have experience in website and application development, e-commerce implementation, commercial photography, graphic design, logo development, and search engine optimization.
