PRSA Member Code of Ethics

Pledge of Professional Conduct

As a practitioner, I pledge to adhere to the core values of the Public Relations Society of America Code of Ethics:

  • Advocacy: Serving as responsible advocates for represented interests.
  • Honesty: Adhering to truth and accuracy in all communications.
  • Expertise: Advancing the profession through ongoing education.
  • Independence: Providing objective counsel to leaders.
  • Loyalty: Faithful to clients while honoring public interest.
  • Fairness: Respecting all stakeholders, peers, and competitors.
