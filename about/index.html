BACKGROUND & EXPERIENCE

About Me

My work brings together storytelling, visual production, and the practical systems behind public programs. In U.S. Army Public Affairs, I worked with Soldiers, civilian staff, schools, community partners, and senior leaders to turn communication needs into finished projects.

Whether interviewing a source, planning a livestream, or coordinating school participation, I focus on clear information, workable processes, and careful follow-through.

My Army experience includes volunteer service and paid employment, listed separately below.

CAREER & COMMUNICATIONS

Background & experience

My background in U.S. Army Public Affairs at Fort Leonard Wood, Missouri, spans volunteer service as a Public Affairs Office Assistant from November 2017 to July 2019 and work as a Public Affairs Specialist from November 2019 to September 2025.

That work has taken me from researching stories and interviewing Soldiers, civilians, and senior leaders to producing photography, video, graphics, and digital content. I contributed to more than 150 published news and feature articles across my Army roles for official Army communication channels, including The Guidon and Army.mil, and produced more than 2,400 official photographs and 50 social videos.

Alongside editorial and visual production, I have coordinated community programs and public-facing engagements involving military units, schools, civic organizations, and government partners. My experience includes developing the Army Values Leadership Award Program, managing Read Across America outreach, and coordinating recurring Scout visits.

I also built the Community Relations Tracker to manage requests and outcomes, and proposed, designed, and built the Show Me Fort Leonard Wood blog during my volunteer service.
