BACKGROUND & EXPERIENCE

About Me

I'm a public relations, communications, and program operations professional with nearly eight years of experience spanning strategic communications, program development and management, journalism, photography, and digital media.

My professional background includes extensive experience with U.S. Army Public Affairs, where my responsibilities extended well beyond traditional communications. I helped develop and manage community relations programs, coordinate complex operations, organize large-scale events, manage requests and resources, and facilitate collaboration among military leadership, community organizations, and external stakeholders.

Alongside my operational responsibilities, I produced award-winning photography, developed digital communications campaigns, created multimedia content, and documented the people, events, and stories that shaped the communities I served.

My work combines creative storytelling with practical execution. I bring the same attention to detail, organizational judgment, and problem-solving abilities to managing programs and coordinating operations that I bring to developing effective communications.

Experience includes volunteer Public Affairs Office Assistant service and full-time Public Affairs Specialist employment.

CAREER & COMMUNICATIONS

Background & experience

My background in U.S. Army Public Affairs at Fort Leonard Wood, Missouri, spans volunteer service as a Public Affairs Office Assistant from November 2017 to July 2019 and work as a Public Affairs Specialist from July 2019 to September 2025.

That work has taken me from researching stories and interviewing Soldiers, civilians, and senior leaders to producing photography, video, graphics, and digital content. I have authored more than 150 news and feature articles for official Army communication channels, including The Guidon and Army.mil, and produced more than 2,400 official photographs.

Alongside editorial and visual production, I have coordinated community programs and public-facing engagements involving military units, schools, civic organizations, and government partners. My experience includes developing the Leadership Award Program, managing Read Across America outreach, and coordinating recurring Scout visits.

I bring both the creative skills to develop communication products and the organizational experience to plan, coordinate, and execute the work behind them.
