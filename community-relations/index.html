STRATEGIC OUTREACH & ENGAGEMENT

Community Relations & Program Operations

Effective community engagement requires more than organizing events. It requires building relationships, understanding the needs of different stakeholders, coordinating resources, and creating opportunities for meaningful participation.

During my career with U.S. Army Public Affairs at Fort Leonard Wood, Missouri, I developed and managed community engagement programs connecting military personnel, government organizations, educational institutions, and surrounding communities.

My responsibilities encompassed program development, stakeholder relations, volunteer recruitment, operational coordination, public communications, and performance evaluation.

CASE STUDY 01

Read Across America

Expanding Educational Outreach Through Community Partnerships

Research & Planning

When I assumed responsibility for coordinating Fort Leonard Wood's Read Across America initiative in 2020, participation was limited to one school district.

Recognizing an opportunity to expand the installation's educational outreach, I developed a scalable approach to increase school participation while maintaining consistent communication and manageable volunteer coordination.

I independently contacted school administrators, established relationships with participating districts, and assessed classroom participation and volunteer requirements.

Implementation

Working with military operations personnel, I coordinated installation-wide volunteer recruitment through formal military tasking procedures.

I served as the primary point of contact for participating Soldiers, managing registration, assignments, scheduling, school locations, and communication with administrators. I also developed recurring email outreach and reminder campaigns to maintain participation and ensure volunteers were prepared.

During COVID-19 restrictions, I adapted the 2021 initiative to virtual delivery, coordinating the production of recorded reading videos. Following each annual event, I compiled participation data, evaluated year-over-year growth, and prepared after-action reports for leadership.

Results & Program Impact

  • 18,000+ Students reached across regional school districts
  • 940 Classrooms served during annual visits
  • 360+ Soldier volunteers coordinated across program years

Expanded participation across regional schools and districts to establish a scalable annual outreach framework. The initiative became one of Fort Leonard Wood's largest recurring community outreach programs.

CASE STUDY 02

Leadership Award Program

Creating a Sustainable Student Recognition Framework

Research & Planning

To build long-term goodwill between the installation and neighboring civilian communities, I recognized a need for a structured, recurring program that recognized student character, leadership, and community service.

I designed the framework for a student Leadership Award Program targeting middle and high school students across central Missouri school districts. The goal was to establish a clear, standardized selection process while maintaining active involvement from senior military leaders.

I collaborated with school superintendents, principals, and counselors to define nomination guidelines, award criteria, and presentation schedules aligned with school academic calendars.

Implementation

I managed end-to-end administration for the program, serving as the central point of contact for school officials across more than 15 school districts.

My operational responsibilities included tracking nominations, preparing official certificates and award materials, coordinating senior military speaker availability, and arranging logistics for in-person award presentations at school assemblies and board meetings.

To ensure continuity, I created standardized administrative templates and scheduling procedures that streamlined annual execution and provided leadership with clear program metrics after each recognition cycle.

Results & Program Impact

  • 200+ Students recognized for outstanding leadership and character
  • 15+ School districts actively participating across regional communities
  • 100% Sustainable annual adoption integrated into military-community operations

Strengthened civilian-military relations across central Missouri by establishing a reliable, high-visibility partnership program that continues to honor youth achievements.
